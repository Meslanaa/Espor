MesOS 0.2 brings MesOS apps and a MesOS-only home screen.

- New MesOS apps: Camera, Photos, Files, Downloads, Calculator, Notes
- MesOS Home shows only MesOS apps, Google Play and apps you installed; turn on Settings > Apps > Show Android apps to see the rest
- New dock: Camera, Photos, Google Play, Settings
- Turkish translation for all of MesOS

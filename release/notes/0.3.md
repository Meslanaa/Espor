MesOS 0.3 "Aurora" is a new MesOS: new look, new home screen and a full set of MesOS apps.

- Aurora design: new fonts, colours, icons for every app, animated Aurora wallpaper
- MesOS Home: pages, widgets (Clock, Weather, Agenda, Notes, Music, Battery and Android widgets), folders, drag and drop, universal search
- Control center (swipe down on Home): quick toggles, brightness, volume, media and notifications
- Setup wizard and a new MesOS Settings with search; MesOS checks for updates once a day
- New apps: Phone, Messages, Contacts, Browser, Clock, Calendar, Weather, Music, Recorder, Scanner, Device Care, Tips
- Photos can edit: crop, rotate, filters and adjustments (saved as a copy)
- Files, Notes and the other MesOS apps use the new design
